## Important caveat on the Estate exemption figure

The `estate-desktop.png` screenshot shows the federal estate tax exemption as **$15M**, while the earlier narrow-viewport pass recorded **$30M** for the same "Current Projections" household. This is very likely because, during the fixture-build work in this session, the shared Current Finances household was switched from Couple to Individual filing status (for the separate fixture plan) — and since Current Finances is shared across all Plans in an account, that change silently altered "Current Projections" too. **This is itself a good piece of evidence for a real product behavior**: the federal estate tax exemption the app displays appears to be filing-status-aware (a single filer's $15M vs. a couple's combined $30M), which is realistic (2026 federal estate tax exemption is ~$15M per individual, doubled for married couples via portability/joint exemption) — not a bug, but a consequence of shared account state plus a correctly-implemented tax rule. Documented here as [OBSERVED, mechanism inferred] rather than corrected away, since it's a genuinely useful data point for Northstar's own estate-tax modeling.
